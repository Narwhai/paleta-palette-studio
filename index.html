AN APPROXIMATION, NOT A GUARANTEE
How optimization works

First, interface roles are separated from BG: BG 2 at 1.04:1, UI at 1.08:1, UI 2 at 1.14:1, and UI 3 at 1.22:1. Text and Text 2 are checked against BG, BG 2, and UI at 7:1 and 4.5:1, respectively. Text 3 at 3:1 and each accent at 4.5:1 are checked against BG and BG 2. UI 2 and UI 3 are treated as borders or details, not text backgrounds. The solver seeks colors no lighter than their checked backgrounds in light mode, and no darker in dark mode. If a target cannot be met in that direction, the selected color is kept; the warning reports both the best directional ratio and the selected color's actual minimum contrast. The contrast check above shows only BG and BG 2. This WCAG-based approach approximates the article’s method, not its exact perceptual algorithm, and is not a universal accessibility guarantee. Check your actual usage.
